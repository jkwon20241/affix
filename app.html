Application

The app does the remembering.

It tells you which strip is today's, picks the site for this week's Stamp, and keeps the record. Here’s a Saturday in the app with nothing logged yet. Go ahead and take today’s strip.

9:41

Try it

  1. Take today's Stick

    On Today, tap Take. The strip dissolves in about ten seconds; here it's sped up.

  2. Stamp this week's site

    Tap Stamp now, then press and hold until it clicks.

  3. Find today in Month

    Tap any day to see what was logged on it.

  4. Change a dose in Me

    Pick a different Stick or Stamp. The rest of the app follows, and so does My month.

What it does

Three jobs, done quietly.

Picks the site

Left arm, right arm, left thigh, right thigh, in turn. Never the same site twice in a row.

Logs both

One tap for the morning strip, one press for the weekly Stamp. The month view shows every day.

Refills on time

Your box ships when the pack runs low, with the next cartridges inside.
