Vitamins you stick and stamp.

Affix replaces pills with two formats: a daily strip that dissolves on the tongue and a weekly Stamp applied to the arm or thigh. The app records both.

Build your monthHow it works

One strip daily. One Stamp weekly.

Daily doses are taken on the tongue. Larger doses are applied to the upper arm or thigh and leave a colored mark that fades within a day.

Tongue · Daily · 10 seconds
Stick

A film strip that dissolves on the tongue in about ten seconds. Take one each morning.

Upper arm or thigh · Weekly · One press
Stamp

A press-on applicator with a color-coded cartridge. Hold it on the site and press until it clicks. The mark fades within a day.

Four steps, managed by the app.

Refills arrive monthly. The app indicates what to take and where, and records each dose.
