Vitamins you stick and stamp.

Affix has no pills. A thin strip melts on your tongue every morning. Once a week, a press-on Stamp on your arm or thigh does the rest. The app keeps track of both.

Two formats

One strip a day. One stamp a week.

Small daily doses go on the tongue. Bigger doses go where shots usually go, and leave a colored mark that fades by tomorrow.

Tongue · daily · about 10 seconds
Stick

A dissolving film strip. Press one onto your tongue in the morning and it's gone before you've put the pack away.

Upper arm or thigh · weekly · one press
Stamp

A press-on applicator with a cartridge in the dose color. Hold it on the site, press until it clicks, lift. The mark fades within a day.

How it works

Four steps, mostly on your phone.

Refills come once a month. The app tells you what to take and where, then keeps the record.
