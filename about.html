The idea

What if vitamins worked like stickers and shots?

Pills rattle, get forgotten and pile up in a drawer. Affix asks what taking a vitamin would feel like if it borrowed from two things people already do without thinking: putting something on the tongue, and getting a quick press on the arm.

Small daily doses become Stick, a film strip that dissolves on the tongue. Bigger, less frequent doses become Stamp, a press-on applicator used on the upper arm or thigh, the places shots usually go. It leaves a small colored mark that fades by the next day.

The app holds it together. It rotates the Stamp between four sites, logs every strip, and sends the refill box before the pack runs out.

The system

Two shapes do all the work.

The strip stands for Stick and the dot stands for Stamp. Together they make the i in the wordmark, the app icon, the patterns and the motion.
