What if vitamins worked like stickers and shots?

Pills are easy to forget and often go unused. Affix borrows from two familiar actions instead: placing something on the tongue and receiving a quick press on the arm.

Small daily doses become Stick, a film strip that dissolves on the tongue. Larger, less frequent doses become Stamp, a press-on applicator for the upper arm or thigh, where injections are usually given. It leaves a small colored mark that fades within a day.

The app connects the system. It rotates the Stamp across four sites, records every strip and ships the refill box before the pack runs out.
